About the press

Books for the Orthodox Christian.

Wonderworker Press publishes Orthodox Christian books made to be used daily — companions for prayer, study, and the household.

We are an independent publishing company based out of Knoxville, Tennessee, and the writers and publisher are part of the canonical Orthodox Church, attending a ROCOR parish. The Press has received the blessings of Metropolitan Nicholas of Eastern America and New York and Bishop Luke of Syracuse.

The Press is dedicated to all the wonderworking saints: St Nicholas the Wonderworker of Myra, St Spyridon the Wonderworker of Trimythous, St Gregory the Wonderworker of Neocaesarea, St Simeon the Wonderworker of the Wondrous Mountain, St Ioasaph the Wonderworker of Belgorod, St Nektarios the Wonderworker of Aegina, St Seraphim the Wonderworker of Sarov, St Sergius the Wonderworker of Radonezh, St Alexander the Wonderworker of Svir, St Tikhon the Wonderworker of Zadonsk, St Mitrophan the Wonderworker of Voronezh, and countless others; and especially to St John the Wonderworker of Shanghai and San Francisco, our family’s patron.
