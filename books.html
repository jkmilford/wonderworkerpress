Saints for Every Need

An Orthodox Companion of Intercessors

This book is for every Orthodox Christian seeking a deeper understanding of — and living connection with — the Body of Christ: the great “cloud of witnesses” that St. Paul describes in Hebrews.

Each theme is alphabetized by need, so you can find what you are praying for in a moment. An Index of Saints and an Index of Needs at the back of the book make finding prayer needs helpful and efficient.

Paperback · 600+ needs · 400+ saints · 9 themes

Saints & Prayers for Wives & Mothers

Expanding on Saints for Every Need, this Orthodox companion walks with women through every stage of life—bride, mother, grandmother, and widow. Inside: dozens of holy wives, mothers, and widows with their troparia, kontakia, and prayers; St. Arsenios’s psalms for every need; wonderworking icons of the Theotokos; and akathists, canons, and the Jesus Prayer, all faithfully translated for lay use.

He Loves Mankind

An Orthodox Perspective on Calvinism & Predestination

Jeremiah Milford

Written for the Orthodox Christian, catechumen, or inquirer who is navigating conversations with Calvinist friends, in-laws, and family members who do not understand the Orthodox teaching on predestination and the basics of the Faith. This book is a useful reference for those seeking a patristic grasp on key Scriptures that are misunderstood by Reformed and Calvinist Protestants.
